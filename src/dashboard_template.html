Return spread = ROIC (after tax) minus cost of capital; for financial industries ROE minus cost of equity. Relative multiple = EV/EBITDA over the median non-financial industry (P/B over the median financial industry for financials). Ranks: share of industries with a strictly lower value (reversed for multiple and beta). Verdicts: Quality at a discount (spread > 0, multiple < 1x), Quality, priced for it (spread > 0, multiple >= 1x), Cheap for a reason, Expensive without returns.
